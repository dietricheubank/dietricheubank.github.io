What an IT operating review should answer

A practical review connects technical service health with decisions leaders need to make.

A useful operating review should not merely report green and red metrics. It should explain whether services are delivering expected outcomes and what leadership action is required.

Four questions

  1. Are our services reliable and secure?
  2. Are we delivering the commitments that matter?
  3. What risks, dependencies and capacity constraints could derail us?
  4. Which decision or escalation is needed now?

Build a consistent rhythm

Use a repeatable scorecard with trends, owners, corrective actions and due dates. Avoid excessive metrics without a decision attached. Start from the public service scorecard template.
