AI infrastructure

From lab demo to reliable service

A working inference endpoint is only the first milestone.

In a lab, success may mean a model responds to a prompt. In daily operation, success means that it recovers, stays observable and does not depend on a particular terminal session.

Measure the whole experience

Separate GPU and model readiness from gateway availability, browser reachability and the quality of model output. That separation prevents unnecessary rebuilds when a problem is really in the network or presentation layer.

Prefer known-good components

Pin and document validated versions. Reevaluate upgrades deliberately, because runtime compatibility depends on model, accelerator, libraries and inter-node communications.

Build a recovery path

Document expected startup order, diagnostics and simple operator commands. After each major change, test what happens when a service fails or a machine restarts. These principles informed my Forge case study.
