Portfolio governance

Weighted scores are a conversation, not a decision

A transparent model makes disagreements visible, but does not eliminate judgment.

A weighted score can help compare initiatives with different benefits and constraints. It makes the assumptions explicit: what matters, how much it matters and how each initiative performs.

What a score can do

Surface priorities, create consistent comparisons and prompt productive questions about strategic fit, urgency and effort.

What it cannot do

It does not guarantee financial return, account for all dependencies or approve work automatically. Data quality and scoring calibration still matter.

Keep humans accountable

Document the criteria, weights and exceptions. Revisit results when strategy changes. Try the public Project Priority Studio with fictional examples.
