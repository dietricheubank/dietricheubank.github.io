# 90-Day Technology Improvement Roadmap

**Objective:** [one measurable outcome] · **Owner:** [role]

## Days 1–30: Discover and baseline
- Meet stakeholders and document user outcomes
- Inventory services, ownership, risks and operational pain points
- Establish baseline metrics and key constraints
- Identify quick fixes versus structural improvements

**Evidence:** Baseline scorecard, prioritized problem list and agreed scope.

## Days 31–60: Stabilize and pilot
- Deliver quick wins with clear rollback plans
- Pilot the highest-impact changes with representative users
- Create runbooks, monitoring and support ownership
- Review progress against outcomes, not activity counts

**Evidence:** Pilot results, risk updates, operational acceptance.

## Days 61–90: Scale and institutionalize
- Scale validated improvements
- Assign durable owners and governance cadence
- Establish monthly operating review and benefits tracking
- Publish next-quarter decisions and tradeoffs

**Evidence:** Accepted operating model, scorecard, roadmap and retrospectives.
