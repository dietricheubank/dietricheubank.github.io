Open tool · Strategic portfolio management

Project Priority Studio

Evaluate initiatives transparently with a configurable weighted scoring model. All calculations run in your browser; no data is uploaded. This is a generic public demonstration, not an organizational policy.

Scoring criteria

Score each criterion from 1 (low) to 5 (high). Weights must total 100%.

Default weights: 30%, 25%, 20%, 15%, 10%. Labels and priority thresholds below are illustrative and editable.

Priority thresholds

Critical ≥ High ≥ Medium ≥

Lower scores are Low. Thresholds must be descending.

Portfolio results

Weighted score = Σ (criterion score × criterion weight) ÷ 100. Higher scores rank first.

Sample projects are fictional. The score measures the criteria you choose—not investment return, clinical safety, or a substitute for governance. No live organizational or patient information should be entered on a shared computer. Methodology and limitations.
