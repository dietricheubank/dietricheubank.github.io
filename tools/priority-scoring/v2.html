Project Priority Studio 2.0

Compare initiatives by weighted strategic priority and estimated effort, then model a simple capacity-limited shortlist. Transparent assumptions. Fictional examples. Entirely local to your browser.

Model controls

Scores: 1–5, higher is better. Weights must sum to 100%.

Effort points are illustrative units, not dollars or hours. The planner uses a greedy priority-per-effort heuristic, not an optimal portfolio solver.

Priority bands

Critical ≥ 4.5 · High ≥ 3.7 · Medium ≥ 2.7 · Low below 2.7. These thresholds are illustrative defaults.

Dependencies are comma-separated initiative IDs. An initiative is selected only after all its dependencies have been selected and if capacity allows.
